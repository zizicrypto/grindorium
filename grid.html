How it works
01There are 500,000 cells. Each one holds exactly one word. Once placed, it is permanent.
02Write in any language. Every word must be unique across the entire grid.
03You can claim one cell per device. Click any empty cell, type your word, and it is yours.
04Drag to move across the grid. Use the zoom buttons or Ctrl+scroll to get closer or further away.
05When all 500,000 cells are filled, one word will appear at the center. No one knows exactly when that will be.
