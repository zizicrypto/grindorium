Your brain was not built for this world.

It was built for scarcity. For silence. For long stretches of nothing. For problems that had edges and solutions you could see.

Now it gets hit with 10,000 stimuli before breakfast. Notifications that require immediate response. Feeds designed by engineers whose job is to make it impossible to look away. News that never resolves. Social comparison at scale never before possible in human history.

Your brain is doing exactly what it was designed to do. It is responding to signals. It is scanning for threat. It is trying to keep up.

The problem is not your brain. The problem is the mismatch between what the brain was built for and what it is now being asked to do.

You are not weak for finding it overwhelming. You are a human nervous system trying to function in an environment nothing in your evolutionary history prepared you for.

The overstimulation is not personal. But the recovery is. And it starts with understanding what your system actually needs.
