You are not behind. You are on a different timeline.

By the time you were supposed to have your career figured out, your relationship stable, your finances sorted. By now you were supposed to feel like an adult. By now it was supposed to make sense.

The 'supposed to' is not real. It is a composite of other people's paths, cultural timelines that were never designed for everyone, and a comparison set that includes no information about what those people's lives actually cost them.

You are not behind. You are on a different timeline. One shaped by what you were given, what you were not given, what happened to you, and what you chose.

The people who appear ahead are not ahead. They are somewhere else on a different path. Comparison across paths tells you nothing useful.

The only timeline that matters is whether today you moved toward something real.
