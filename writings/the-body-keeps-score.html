Your body has been trying to tell you something for a long time.

The tension in your shoulders you have learned to ignore. The jaw you clench at night. The shallow breathing that is just how you breathe now. The stomach that tightens at certain messages before you have even read them.

Your body has been trying to tell you something for a long time. You have gotten very good at not listening.

The body keeps record of what the mind has decided to file away. Stress that was never discharged. Grief that had nowhere to go. Fear that was swallowed instead of moved through. It does not forget.

This is not metaphor. Chronic stress and unprocessed emotion have measurable physiological effects, on the nervous system, the immune system, the cardiovascular system, the quality of sleep.

You are not imagining the physical symptoms. You are experiencing the cost of carrying more than you have been acknowledging.

The body is not betraying you. It is asking you to pay attention.
