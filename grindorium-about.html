About

What Grindorium is.

Most self-improvement content tells you to wake up earlier, do more, push harder. Grindorium does not.

Grindorium is for the people who are already exhausted. The ones who are functional on the outside and running on empty on the inside. The ones who know what they should be doing and cannot make themselves do it. The ones who have read all the advice and still feel stuck.

This is not a productivity platform. It is a self-awareness platform.

The assessments

The tests here are built on psychological research - burnout, attachment theory, emotional regulation, self-esteem, procrastination. They are designed to tell you something true about yourself, not just give you a label. Each one measures two dimensions and gives you one of nine possible results, each with its own analysis and specific next steps.

Burnout↗ Emotional Numbness↗ Anxiety and Overstimulation↗ Attachment Style↗ Discipline↗ Perfectionism↗ People Pleasing↗ Loneliness↗ Self-Sabotage↗ Emotional Maturity↗ Stress↗ Self-Esteem↗ Procrastination↗

The essays

The writings are for people who are tired of being told to optimize. They are about silence, about what drives avoidance, about the relationship you have with yourself when no one is watching. No listicles. No bullet points. No ten-step frameworks.

Just honest writing about the things most people are experiencing but not talking about.

The videos

The YouTube channel follows the same principle. Cinematic, quiet, direct. No shouting. No hacks. No false promises. Short films and longer essays on the psychology of modern life - overstimulation, discipline, emotional numbness, self-sabotage.

Grindorium was built for the person who already knows something is wrong. Who is looking not for motivation but for clarity.

You are safe here.
