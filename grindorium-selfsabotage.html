Self-Sabotage Assessment

Are you your own worst enemy?

You know what to do. You do not do it. Or you get close to something good and then you find a way to destroy it. Or you start strong and disappear before the finish line.

This assessment measures three dimensions of self-sabotage: fear-based avoidance, self-worth sabotage, and behavioral self-defeat.

12 questions · 4 to 5 minutes · Based on self-handicapping and fear of success research · Not a clinical diagnostic tool
