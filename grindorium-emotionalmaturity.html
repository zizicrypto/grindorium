Emotional Maturity Assessment

How emotionally mature are you, really?

Age is not emotional maturity. You can be 40 and still respond to disappointment like you are 14. You can be 25 and have more emotional stability than most people twice your age.

This assessment measures three dimensions: emotional regulation, accountability, and relational maturity. It will not tell you what you want to hear.

12 questions · 4 to 5 minutes · Based on emotional intelligence and DBT research · Not a clinical diagnostic tool
