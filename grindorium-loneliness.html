Loneliness Assessment

Are you lonely in a room full of people?

Loneliness is not about how many people are around you. It is about whether any of them actually know you. Whether you are actually known.

This assessment measures two dimensions: social loneliness and existential loneliness, the feeling of being fundamentally unseen.

10 questions · 3 to 4 minutes · Based on UCLA loneliness scale and Cacioppo research · Not a clinical diagnostic tool
